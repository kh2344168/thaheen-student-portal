# Learning ideas I would explore next

These are ideas for a future product, not features in this offline screening app. They come from my education background and from discussing a separate English-learning project with teachers. I would start by speaking with Thaheen students and instructors before building any of them.

1. **Check understanding during long videos.** At topic boundaries chosen with an instructor, pause for one short question about the concept just explained. Let the student replay that part if needed. I would test whether these checkpoints help students recall the material; time spent playing a video alone does not prove understanding.
2. **Offer an interactive recap after each lesson.** Ask a few focused questions and use the answers to revisit confusing points, with a link back to the relevant moment in the video. This could connect the recorded lesson to Thaheen's existing exam-style practice without repeating the whole lecture.
3. **Let students explain what they learned.** Give them a choice to speak or write a short explanation, then receive feedback on what they understood and what needs another look. I would prototype feedback against instructor-approved answers, test it with teachers for accuracy, and make recording optional. An AI-backed version would need a secure service beyond this offline task.

I would prototype the first idea with a small group of students, ask instructors to review the questions, and compare feedback and recall before deciding whether to add the others.
